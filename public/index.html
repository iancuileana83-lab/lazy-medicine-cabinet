      What it does

      Take a photo of your medicine boxes. A Gemma model running on this laptop reads the product name and expiry date, and the app shows which ones are expired or running out.

      Privacy promise

      Nothing leaves this laptop. No cloud services, no accounts, no analytics. The list you see is saved in a file on this computer, together with a copy of each photo you scan, so you can see where each line came from. The photos stay only on this laptop. They are deleted when the line is removed, or with Clear all.

      How it works

      1. You choose a photo.
      2. The local model copies the name and date as printed.
      3. The app works out the status.
      4. You check and fix each line.

      Limits

      Lazy Medicine Cabinet can misread labels. Always check each line against the real box. This tool gives no medical advice, doses or interaction information. Ask a pharmacist or doctor about your medicines.

      Month-only dates are treated as the end of that month.
